EXP009-C3-B2 Safe-Subset Beneficial Opportunity Audit
=====================================================

Research question
-----------------
C3-B1.3 showed that Harmful fate is observable, but perfect harm avoidance explains only a minority of the TrialOracle gap.
C3-B2 therefore asks: among safe / non-harmful trials, which ones are actually worth rescuing?

Exact decomposition
-------------------
Beneficial = CheapFailure AND FallbackSuccess

This motivates separate CheapFailure and Rescueability models, plus a factorized Beneficial score:
  pB_factor = p(CheapFailure|X) * p(FallbackSuccess|CheapFailure,X)

C3-B1.3 fixed harm-veto threshold = 0.07189384
Extended lambda sanity optimum = 19.2500

Interpretation logic
--------------------
1. If CheapFailure AUC is much higher than direct Beneficial AUC, the first missing piece is cheap-branch reliability estimation.
2. If Rescueability AUC is weak even after conditioning on CheapFailure, fallback success itself is the harder latent variable.
3. If factorized Beneficial outperforms direct Beneficial inside the OracleSafe subset, the decomposition is algorithmically useful.
4. If factorized+HarmVeto improves matched-budget recovery, the full three-stage policy (state opportunity -> harm veto -> rescue ranking) gets its first positive pilot.
5. If all safe-subset Beneficial scores remain near 0.5, the next move should not be a bigger classifier; we need new observables or a more direct model of cheap failure / fallback rescue physics.
