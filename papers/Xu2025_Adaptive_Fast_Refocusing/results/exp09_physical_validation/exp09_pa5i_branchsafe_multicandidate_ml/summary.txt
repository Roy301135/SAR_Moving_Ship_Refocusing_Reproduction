Interpretation discipline
-------------------------
1) G4 is a diagnostic global reference, not a proposed practical estimator.
2) No gate threshold is tuned in PA5I.
3) Branch recovery is evaluated against G4, not against a pre-assumed true mixture bias.
4) A practical method must be judged jointly by catastrophic failure rate and computational cost.
5) PA5I does not select the strong-removal window.
