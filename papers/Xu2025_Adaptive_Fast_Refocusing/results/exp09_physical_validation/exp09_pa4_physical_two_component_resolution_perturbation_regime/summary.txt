Interpretation discipline
-------------------------
1) Do not call a case "NearShift" unless a weak-associated observable total peak actually exists.
2) Gamma is the primary separation coordinate; absolute beta/q differences are secondary.
3) First-order perturbation is evaluated only where a weak peak exists and the predicted shift is locally bounded.
4) Failure of old normalized A4 under physical parameters is a valid result; do not tune parameters to preserve the old mechanism.
5) PA4 is noiseless and pre-CLEAN. Noise/CLEAN are reintroduced only after the physical topology is established.
