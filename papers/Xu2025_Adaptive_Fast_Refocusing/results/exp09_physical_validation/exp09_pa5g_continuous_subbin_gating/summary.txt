EXP009 PA5G / Continuous Sub-Bin Estimation + Gating
===================================================

Purpose
-------
1) remove PA5F deterministic interpolation bias with a continuous ML/LS estimator;
2) test a data-driven gate against mixture-induced false correction.

Physical setup
--------------
lambda=0.1 m, R0=4242.64 m, PRF=188 Hz
eta=[0 0.125 0.25 0.375 0.5] bins
windows=[1 3 5 9 17] bins

Continuous estimator
--------------------
nu_hat = argmax_nu |sum z[n] exp(-j2pi nu n/N)|^2
search halfwidth=0.75 bins, bracket points=33, TolX=1e-11 bins

Evidence gate
-------------
Trigger iff DeltaBIC>0 AND |fractional nu_hat| > split-aperture disagreement.
No physical threshold is tuned.

Interpretation discipline
-------------------------
1) Continuous ML/LS is model-derived, not selected after viewing the PA5G outcome.
2) The BIC penalty is fixed by model dimension; the split condition uses internal disagreement, not a tuned threshold.
3) G2 is not practical because it has access to strong-only data. It isolates estimator bias.
4) G4 is the practical deterministic candidate.
5) Noise, clutter and strong-chirp-rate estimation error remain OFF until this deterministic gate is audited.
