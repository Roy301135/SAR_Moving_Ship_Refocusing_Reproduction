Interpretation discipline
-------------------------
1) LocalMax is the legacy implementation, not asserted as the authors' exact private FindPeak semantics.
2) ConnectedSupport and PlateauAware are implementation audits, not retroactive claims about Wang 2023.
3) Window-coordinate collapse is evaluated on the same actual operators; PA5E does not create a new equal-Hz operator family.
4) Pareto dominance replaces the arbitrary 90% balanced-window selection rule.
5) E<=1 remains a diagnostic waveform-feasibility threshold, not a universal task-performance threshold.
6) A nonzero rmin that survives tie-safe semantics is evidence for a genuine fence-aware recoverability floor in this bridge.
7) Noise remains OFF until this audit is closed.
